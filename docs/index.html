About

Residential indicators for every West Virginia county, built for West Virginia University and modeled on the Upper Peninsula Regional Economic Report. Commercial real estate is out of scope: no free county-level data exists.

Sources

  • Census ACS 5-year (55 counties)
  • FHFA House Price Index (41 counties)
  • Realtor.com via FRED (12 counties)
  • Census Building Permits · HUD FMR

Method

Coverage gaps are shown, never filled. County boundaries from U.S. Census via plotly. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any listed source. Accessibility target: WCAG 2.1 AA.
